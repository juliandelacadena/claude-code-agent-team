# Financial update

1. Read `data/financials.md` and `data/inventory.md`; restate the current numbers and their
   date.
2. Ask for any new figures since the last update (sales, ad spend, new costs, stock changes).
3. Update unit economics (price, COGS, margin).
4. Refresh the path-to-sell-through model: units × margin vs. spend, with assumptions listed.
5. Flag the single biggest risk (cash, margin, or inventory) and the top lever.
6. Write updated numbers back to `data/financials.md` **only after the founder confirms.**
